Levering betas: the pure-play method

Unlever each comparable's beta to remove its financial risk, average the asset betas, then relever at your company's capital structure (Lecture 4, slides 23 to 26).

Comparable companies

βasset = βequity / [1 + (1 − t)(D/E)] and βequity = βasset × [1 + (1 − t)(D/E)], assuming a debt beta of zero (slide 25).

Relevered equity betaYour target D/E

Textbook example: Thatsit Company (beta 1.4, D/E 0.5, tax 40%) gives an asset beta of 1.0769 and a project beta of 1.2385 at D/E 0.25. GSK and Haleon: the household-products asset beta of 0.74 (Damodaran, January 2026) relevers to 0.88 at D/E 0.25 and 25% tax, as on slide 26.
